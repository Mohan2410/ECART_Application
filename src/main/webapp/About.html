About ECART

A simpler way to manage an electronics storefront.

ECART is an electronic-store application that brings product browsing and store administration together in one place.

For customers

Browse and purchase

Customers can create an account, sign in, view the available catalog, and submit a purchase request based on the inventory shown by the application.

Customer sign in

For administrators

Maintain the catalog

Authorized administrators can add products, review the catalog, and edit or remove product records through the management pages.
